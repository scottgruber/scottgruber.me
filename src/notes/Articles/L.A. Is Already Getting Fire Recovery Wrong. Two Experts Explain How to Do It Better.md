- Summary: Two wildfire experts, Stephen Pyne and Jack Cohen, believe that the recent destructive fires in Los Angeles could have been less severe with better planning and building practices. They argue that communities need to focus on preventing home ignitions by addressing vulnerabilities rather than just firefighting efforts. A more comprehensive understanding of fire as a constant risk, rather than a seasonal emergency, is essential for developing effective strategies to live with it.
